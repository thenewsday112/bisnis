#293. DoorDash to acquire Instacart for $17 billion

📅 Oct 28, 2026  |  Category: Business  |  Read Source →

DoorDash will acquire Instacart for about $17 billion, the companies announced Thursday, uniting two delivery giants under one roof.

DoorDash will pay $65 a share in cash and stock, an 11% premium to Instacart's closing price on Wednesday, and about 26% above Instacart's closing price on Oct. 22, the last trading day before reports surfaced of a possible sale.

Shares of DoorDash jumped more than 6% in morning trading following news of the deal, while Instacart traded near the flatline.

The deal is slated to close at the end of 2027 or early 2028, the companies said. DoorDash expects to retain Instacart's brand, enterprise technology products and app once the deal closes.

DoorDash CEO Tony Xu said in a statement that grocery is "one of the hardest things to get right online." He pointed to difficulties around picking the right inventory and delivering perishable goods to customers' doorsteps without damaging them on the way.

"Instacart has spent years learning to do that well for grocers, while DoorDash has built a delivery network and technology that local stores rely on every day," Xu said. "Together, we could give stores of every size more of those tools and unlock the potential of grocery delivery."

DoorDash reported its third-quarter results alongside the deal announcement. Adjusted earnings per share were $1.06, beating the average analyst estimate of 84 cents per share, according to LSEG. Revenue came in at $4.62 billion, topping LSEG estimates of $4.56 billion.

Marketplace gross order value, which tracks the total dollar value of orders on its platform, grew 36% year over year to $33.9 billion, beating consensus estimates of $33.6 billion. Total orders reached 993 million, up 28% from a year ago.

Speculation of a deal has swirled since last week, when The Information reported Instacart was exploring a sale and had held talks with companies including Uber and DoorDash. On Monday, several outlets reported DoorDash was in advanced discussions to acquire Instacart and that the deal could value Instacart at about $17 billion.

Sources familiar with the matter had told CNBC on Wednesday that the deal could be announced as soon as Thursday morning.

Acquiring Instacart would give DoorDash a bigger presence in online grocery delivery. DoorDash got its start in food delivery, and since then, it has added more retailers to its marketplace, such as Sephora, Macy's, Staples and Anthropologie. It's recently made grocery a bigger priority, adding a stable of regional grocers after announcing a partnership with Kroger last year.

Instacart, which went public in 2023, was one of the biggest beneficiaries of a pandemic-driven surge in grocery delivery services, but it has struggled to maintain that momentum. In December Instacart agreed to pay $60 million to settle allegations by the Federal Trade Commission that it misled users with false advertising and deployed "unlawful subscription enrollment" practices.

The service has also faced increasingly stiff competition from companies like Amazon and Walmart. After slowing its brick-and-mortar grocery effort, Amazon has doubled down on its online grocery business and leveraged its vast fulfillment footprint to speed up deliveries of household essentials, pantry staples and perishables.

Both Amazon and Walmart have pushed further into same- and next-day delivery, which poses a threat to Instacart and DoorDash's on-demand delivery model.

Analysts have noted that a potential combination of Instacart and DoorDash could attract antitrust scrutiny given the companies' market overlap.

Third-party delivery platforms "remain a minority of total grocery consumption, but slicing and dicing the online market more narrowly or from a perspective of the grocers as enterprise customers can make approvals more challenging," Wedbush analysts wrote in a Monday research note.

📅 Oct 28, 2026  |  Category: Technology  |  Read Source →

The UK may have no involvement in the US-Iran war, but the country is feeling the full force of the conflict on its economy.

Soaring energy prices are set to dent growth in the short term, push up inflation and leave living standards in a "grim" place, according to the latest official forecast from the Office for Budget Responsibility (OBR).

Interest rates are also predicted to rise, making borrowing more expensive, along with government debt repayments.

Chancellor John Healey warned the nation faces "real challenges ahead" as he announced policies that he said would offer "breathing space" for households in the coming months.

"What we face today are global economic storms," he said. "We can't wish them away. We must choose the course we steer through them."

In its first assessment of the UK economy since the Middle East conflict started, the OBR said rising energy prices were the biggest risk to the economy in the near future.

Despite raising its growth forecast for this year, it sharply cut its prediction for next year from 1.6% to 1%.

Growth is then set to rebound in the following years.

When an economy grows, businesses tend to invest more, create more jobs and give pay rises to workers, which leaves people feeling better off on average, though it can take time and not everyone necessarily benefits.

Companies making higher profits also pay more in tax, which can enable the government to spend more on public services. When there is slow economic growth, or the economy shrinks, the opposite can occur.

Inflation can weigh on growth and the OBR has forecast that prices are set to rise at a faster pace as higher energy bills filter through supply chains and to consumers, especially in 2027.

The OBR now thinks inflation will stay higher for longer than previously forecast, peaking at 3.8% in 2027.

Despite the cost-of-living support announced by the government, economists at Pantheon Macroeconomics said the measures were "small beer given the surge in inflation we expect over the winter".

High inflation paints a bleak picture when it comes to living standards and the OBR has forecast a dip in the growth in living standards next year as energy bills hit the spending power of households.

Those on lower incomes are set to be hit harder, however, because energy costs eat up a bigger share of their monthly budget and disposable income.

Real household disposable income, a measure of living standards, is forecast to grow from 2027, but not by as much as previously thought.

#495. Premi Risiko RI Melonjak! Awas Rupiah Kian Melemah-Utang Makin Mahal

Jakarta, CNBC Indonesia - Credit Default Swap (CDS) atau premi risiko investasi Indonesia terus mengalami kenaikan. Berdasarkan data revinitif levelnya sudah melampaui 100 sejak 22 Oktober 2026, dan ke level 106,34 pada 27 Oktober 2026 untuk tenor 5 tahun. Padahal, pada 14 Oktober 2026 masih di level 91,78.

Kepala Ekonom BCA David Sumual mengatakan, tren kenaikan CDS tenor 5 tahun ini masih disebabkan kekhawatiran investor terhadap pengumuman MSCI. Pada 15 Oktober 2026, MSCI mengumumkan dimulainya konsultasi mengenai usulan untuk mengubah klasifikasi pasar saham Indonesia dari emerging market menjadi standalone market.

"Masih terkait MSCI shock," kata David kepada CNBC Indonesia, Rabu (28/10/2026).

Selain itu, David mengatakan naiknya premi risiko itu juga dipicu oleh faktor eksternal, mulai seperti harga minyak yang cenderung masih tinggi, imbas konflik di Timur Tengah yang kian memburuk. Harga minyak mentah Brent naik US$1,48 atau 1,3 persen menjadi US$115,36 per barel pada pukul 01.40 GMT. Sementara itu, harga minyak mentah West Texas Intermediate (WTI) naik US$1,22 atau 1,22 persen menjadi US$101,12 per barel.

"Harga minyak masih cenderung tinggi sehingga pasar obligasi domestik tampak khawatir," kata David.

David memperingatkan, tingginya level CDS tenor benchmark ini berisiko meningkatkan imbal hasil surat utang pemerintah Indonesia. Selain itu, juga akan terus menekan nilai tukar dolar rupiah terhadap dolar Amerika Serikat (AS).

"Dampaknya pada harga obligasi dan tekanannya terhadap kurs rupiah," papar David.

Senada, Kepala Ekonom Permata Bank Josua Pardede mengatakan, kenaikan CDS Indonesia tenor 5 tahun yang bertahan di atas 100 basis poin sejak 22 Oktober hingga mencapai sekitar 106,09 basis poin mencerminkan meningkatnya persepsi risiko investor terhadap Indonesia akibat kombinasi tekanan global dan ketidakpastian domestik.

Dari sisi global, ia mengatakan, ketegangan geopolitik Timur Tengah yang mempertahankan harga minyak di atas 115 dolar AS per barel meningkatkan kekhawatiran terhadap inflasi, prospek suku bunga Amerika Serikat, dan keberlanjutan fiskal negara pengimpor minyak seperti Indonesia.

Penguatan dolar AS serta imbal hasil US Treasury tenor 10 tahun yang berada di sekitar 5% juga mendorong investor menuntut kompensasi risiko lebih tinggi untuk memegang aset negara berkembang.

Dari sisi domestik, ancaman penurunan klasifikasi pasar saham Indonesia oleh MSCI turut memperburuk persepsi terhadap kualitas tata kelola pasar keuangan dan kredibilitas kebijakan.

Tekanan rupiah yang mendekati Rp18.200 per dolar AS semakin meningkatkan kekhawatiran mengenai risiko fiskal, kebutuhan valuta asing, dan biaya pembiayaan eksternal.

"Kondisi tersebut menjelaskan mengapa CDS Indonesia meningkat dari sekitar 92,4 basis poin pada pertengahan Oktober menjadi di atas 100 basis poin," paparnya.

Meski begitu, Josua mengingatkan, CDS sebesar 106,09 basis poin tidak berarti probabilitas Indonesia mengalami gagal bayar mencapai 1,06%. Angka tersebut mencerminkan premi perlindungan risiko kredit yang juga dipengaruhi ketidakpastian pasar dan tingkat kehati-hatian investor.

Indonesia kata dia masih memiliki peringkat kredit layak investasi dan cadangan devisa sekitar 146,3 miliar dolar AS. Dengan demikian, kenaikan CDS lebih tepat dibaca sebagai meningkatnya premi risiko Indonesia, bukan indikasi bahwa Indonesia sedang menghadapi krisis gagal bayar.

Ia juga mengakui, CDS yang bertahan tinggi memang meningkatkan risiko kenaikan yield obligasi pemerintah, tetapi tidak berarti yield SUN pasti terus meningkat.

"Keduanya sama-sama dipengaruhi persepsi risiko investor, tetapi pergerakan yield SUN berdenominasi rupiah juga ditentukan oleh ekspektasi inflasi, arah suku bunga BI, kondisi likuiditas perbankan, kebutuhan penerbitan utang pemerintah, serta prospek nilai tukar," papar Josua.

Menurut Josua, tekanan premi risiko sudah terlihat dari kenaikan yield SUN tenor 10 tahun ke sekitar 7,62% pada 28 Oktober, meningkat sekitar 48 basis poin dibandingkan akhir September.

"Selisihnya terhadap US Treasury tenor yang sama mencapai sekitar 261 basis poin, tetapi premi tersebut belum tentu cukup menarik bagi investor asing ketika risiko pelemahan rupiah masih tinggi," ujar Josua.

Pelemahan permintaan pada lelang SUN 27 Oktober, ketika total penawaran turun sekitar 52% dibandingkan lelang dua minggu sebelumnya, menurut Josua juga menunjukkan investor semakin selektif dan meminta imbal hasil lebih tinggi.

Meski demikian, arus modal asing ke SBN selama Oktober kata dia masih mencatatkan pembelian bersih sekitar 480 juta dolar AS.

"Artinya, kenaikan yield lebih mencerminkan penyesuaian harga terhadap peningkatan risiko daripada hilangnya kepercayaan seluruh investor terhadap obligasi pemerintah," kata Josua.

Apabila tekanan global berlanjut, CDS menurut Josua akan terus meningkat, dan rupiah semakin melemah, yield SUN tenor 10 tahun berisiko bergerak mendekati 7,8-8,0%.

Sebaliknya, apabila harga minyak menurun, imbal hasil US Treasury terkoreksi, dan stabilitas rupiah membaik, yield SUN berpeluang kembali turun meskipun CDS belum sepenuhnya kembali ke level sebelumnya.

"Menurut saya, prioritas pemerintah dan BI saat ini adalah menurunkan premi risiko Indonesia melalui penguatan kredibilitas kebijakan, bukan sekadar menahan kenaikan yield SBN melalui intervensi pasar," kata Josua.

Josua menyarankan supaya pemerintah memberikan kepastian mengenai pengelolaan defisit APBN, ketahanan fiskal terhadap kenaikan harga minyak, serta strategi pembiayaan utang yang tidak memaksakan penerbitan SBN pada biaya terlalu tinggi.

BI ia anggap juga perlu melanjutkan stabilisasi rupiah melalui intervensi valuta asing yang terukur, menjaga kecukupan likuiditas perbankan, serta mendukung kelancaran pasar SBN sesuai kewenangannya.

Sementara itu, OJK dan BEI perlu mempercepat reformasi transparansi kepemilikan saham, peningkatan kualitas saham beredar di publik, dan penegakan hukum untuk memulihkan kepercayaan investor global.

"Indikator yang perlu dicermati adalah apakah CDS dapat kembali turun secara konsisten ke bawah 100 basis poin, rupiah mulai stabil, pembelian asing di SBN meningkat, dan permintaan lelang kembali membaik," ungkap Josua.

Ia pun menekankan bahwa CDS di atas 100 basis poin merupakan sinyal peringatan bahwa investor meminta kompensasi risiko lebih besar untuk berinvestasi di Indonesia. Jika tidak segera direspons melalui kebijakan yang kredibel, tekanan tersebut dapat meningkatkan biaya utang pemerintah dan mempersempit ruang fiskal.

"Namun, kenaikan yield lebih lanjut bukan sesuatu yang tidak dapat dihindari karena masih sangat bergantung pada perkembangan risiko global dan keberhasilan pemulihan kepercayaan investor terhadap Indonesia," papar Josua.
