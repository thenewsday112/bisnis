#123. Sam Altman has ‘chronic disappointment’ with MBA students. Here’s his advice for young job seekers

📅 Oct 29, 2026  |  Category: Technology  |  Read Source →

Sam Altman knows how to play the startup game. Beyond co-founding two companies—the social network Loopt, at just 20 years old, and OpenAI, which now stands as one of the highest valued unicorns of all time—he’s helped launch dozens of other startups as the former president of Y Combinator.

That background has led Altman to his view of the current startup landscape: “chronic disappointment.”

In an interview with Stanford’s Graduate School of Business, Altman sounded off on how he feels prospective founders are falling behind in the era of artificial intelligence.

“My chronic disappointment with MBA students is that they always want to go do the thing that was cool five years ago rather than try something new,” he said. “I think this is a great time to go do the new thing.”

What would succumbing to the cool factor look like? Altman says now would be a bad time to “start another copy of OpenAI,” but that “there are many companies that can be built on top of technology that OpenAI has put out into the world that will be much, I hope, bigger and more important than OpenAI.”

Dodging the doomerism

Though Altman has admitted that AI could lead to the apocalypse, he’s quick to shut down a different kind of doomsday: that AI will decimate the job market. 

“I don’t think there’s going to be, like, a job apocalypse in the way that many people talk about,” Altman said. As with finding success in starting a new tech company, he simply encourages job seekers to adapt, rather than stay stuck in the past.

“It is totally true that huge numbers of tasks that are done today by people will be done better, cheaper, faster, completely differently by AI. And it’s also true that wide swaths of current jobs—the way they’re done today, at least—will be entirely done by AI,” Altman continued. 

But that degree of change in any given position’s day-to-day operations is an opportunity, he argues, not a setback.

“If you are heading into the world right now to go work on something, I think you want to be starting your career at a time of the highest degree of change, and you want the most open playing field,” he said. “I think people with high potential and drive and ideas and sort of a new way of looking at things and new skills do relatively the best when the ground is shaking the most.”

The school of startups

Altman may have hot takes on MBAs, but he never received a degree himself, having dropped out of Stanford University after two years of studying computer science in order to found Loopt. But that didn’t stop him from getting what he considers to be an MBA’s worth of education.

“I was sad to drop out of school because I loved school, and I was also worried I just was not going to keep learning stuff,” Altman said. “And one of the coolest things for me about doing a startup is, it was—and still is—the highest rate of learning environment I had ever been in.”

Addressing the room of Stanford students, Altman passed on a piece of wisdom he received as a young startup founder: “You will get an entire MBA in one month of doing a startup.”

“I don’t have an MBA, so I can’t say whether or not that’s true. But I did learn a lot very quickly about business and product and customers and sales and all of this other stuff that has been very important to the rest of my career, that I think I just wouldn’t have been able to learn in school,” he added.

#133. Millions of Americans could be owed up to $280 from a major credit bureau. Here’s who qualifies

📅 Oct 29, 2026  |  Category: Other  |  Read Source →

Consumer credit reporting giant Equifax has agreed to pay $100 million to settle a class action lawsuit over claims it miscalculated millions of credit scores. 

The lawsuit, brought by Gibbs Mura and co-counsel, was originally filed in the U.S. District Court for the Northern District of Georgia in 2022. According to the suit, a coding error led to inaccurate credit report information between March 17 and April 6, 2022, impacting around 4 million Americans who applied for mortgages, auto loans, or credit cards during the aforementioned time frame.

The suit contends that the miscalculation led to consumers receiving lower credit scores on their reports. In some cases, scores dropped by up to 20 points—enough to cause lenders to reject applicants who may have otherwise qualified for the desired loan or line of credit, the suit said.

Equifax has not admitted fault in the case and has denied it violated the Fair Credit Reporting Act (FCRA). Still, the credit reporting company and the plaintiffs chose to settle the case in order “to avoid the risk, expense, and delay of further litigation, including trial and appeals,” according to the settlement website.

David Berger, co-lead counsel and partner at Gibbs Mura, noted in a press release that the settlement is the largest in history related to the FCRA, and said the prosecution is “thrilled to have achieved this result for consumers after nearly four years of litigation.” He added: “Credit reporting agencies must be held accountable when they fail to take commonsense measures handling data that affects people’s livelihoods.”

While the court has preliminarily approved the proposed settlement amount, a final fairness hearing will take place on January 22, 2027. 

How to file a claim

If your credit report was impacted by the alleged error, you should have already received a notice in your email or from the U.S. Postal Service. Check your spam folder if you did not receive a letter.

If you believe you are among the 4 million Americans who may be eligible for part of the $100 million settlement, you can file a claim through Equifax’s settlement website by clicking on the “File a Claim” button. According to the site, you must submit a claim in order to receive a payment. 

How much can I expect?

Payments are expected to be anywhere from $95 to $280 for class members who submit a valid and timely claim; however, actual payments could be higher or lower depending on the number of claims submitted. Payments will be distributed on a pro rata basis from the net settlement fund after court-approved fees, expenses, and administration costs are deducted.

When do I have to submit a claim?

In order to receive a payout from the settlement, your claim must be submitted by December 28, 2026. If for any reason you wish to object or opt out of the settlement, the final date to do so is November 27, 2026. If you are excluded from the settlement, you will get no payment but will maintain the right to bring your own lawsuit against Equifax over the coding issue. 

To request more information or ask questions, contact the settlement administrator at info@EquifaxFairCreditReportingSettlement.com.

Init-6 merupakan perusahaan modal ventura yang berfokus pada perusahaan tahap awal (early stage company) dan didirikan oleh dua pendiri Bukalapak, Achmad Zaky dan Nugroho Herucahyono.

Sementara itu, Netra Runtime merupakan perusahaan infrastruktur inferensi AI asal Indonesia yang membantu perusahaan menjalankan model AI melalui GPU milik sendiri maupun layanan cloud.

AI kini tidak hanya digunakan untuk menjawab pertanyaan, tetapi juga menulis kode, membaca dokumen dalam jumlah besar, hingga menjalankan tugas secara terus-menerus sebagai agen.

#119. Reddit shares sink on weak sales guidance and plans login requirement test as Google search traffic shrinks

📅 Oct 29, 2026  |  Category: Technology  |  Read Source →

Reddit plans to require all users to create official accounts to access the social media platform, marking a major departure for the site known as "the front page of the internet."

The company revealed the change Thursday as part of its third-quarter earnings, in which it beat on the top and bottom lines, but provided weak fourth-quarter sales guidance. The company's shares dropped over 15% after its numbers were released.

Here's how the company did compared with LSEG estimates:

Reddit CEO Steve Huffman told CNBC in an interview that the company's shrinking search-referral traffic from Google went "down meaningfully in this quarter" due to the "shift to AI mode and AI overviews." The traffic decline played a big role in Reddit's planned efforts to require people to become logged-in users.

A previous Google search update designed to surface "authentic" content from internet forums helped Reddit grow its user base over the past few years, but the search giant's continued push on AI overviews and related chatting features has negatively impacted referral traffic.

"I think fundamentally that relationship between Google and the rest of the internet is evolving, and so for us, this means a continued focus on our core users that come to Reddit directly," Huffman said.

Previous Google algorithm changes would "kind of come and go," Huffman said, but the company's AI efforts represent "a fundamental shift in how the Google product works, from being a traffic driver to whatever's going on now."

The CEO didn't provide any specific timing on when Reddit plans for the platform registration change, saying only that implementing it through the site would involve a lot of complexity. The company will begin testing in the fourth quarter outside of the U.S.

He said that Reddit is still determining what parts of the service logged-out users will be able to see, and will iron out the details as part of its upcoming testing.

Reddit said that daily active uniques, or DAUq, rose 12% year-over-year to 130.3 million in the third quarter, trailing Wall Street estimates of 133.7 million. U.S.-specific DAUq dropped 2% year-over-year to 50.7 million, which was below analyst projections of 53.5 million.

Huffman said that "search referral traffic is down meaningfully in this quarter" and that the trend will continue in the fourth quarter.

He said that Reddit is continuing its effort to make its platform "a daily habit" for users. "So that's where our focus is: growing those app users and logged-in users," Huffman said.

Reddit, which derives the bulk of its overall sales from online advertising, is able to monetize logged-ln users better than logged-out users, because those registered users are more active and valuable to advertisers itching to deliver more targeted ads.

"The vast majority of our revenue comes from our app users and logged-in users are direct traffic, and both of those are up Q. over Q. and so that's where our focus is," Huffman said.

The company's third-quarter sales grew 53% year-over-year, marking an end to the eight-straight quarters of revenue growth eclipsing 60% that it reported in July as part of its second-quarter earnings.

Reddit said that fourth-quarter sales will come in a range of $1 billion to $1.03 billion, which was below Wall Street estimates of $1.05 billion.

"It's still a strong guide," Huffman said about its revenue guidance. "We are still growing at an industry leading rate, and that's despite the headwinds from search referrals."

Reddit's third-quarter average revenue per user, or ARPU, came in at $6.87, which was higher than the $6.50 that analysts were expecting.

The company's "other revenue" segment, which includes its data-licensing business, jumped 35% year-over-year to $48 million in the third quarter. Reddit's two biggest data-licensing partners are OpenAI and Google.

Huffman said that Reddit remains "in negotiations" with Google over its data-licensing agreement, and said that it is "only going to do a deal that makes sense for Reddit," if it chooses to continue the partnership.

"I think there's a lot of strategic considerations that goes into that," Huffman said.

Huffman called the required registration plan a "big change" for himself, considering he "grew up on the open Internet."

"Reddit is the last platform to provide basically unfettered logged-out access, and that's largely because of me, I've been holding that line," Huffman, a Reddit co-founder, said. "I think users will probably go through the same understanding that I have, which is that it's a sign of the times. It's an evolution of how these platforms work."

Huffman later expanded on the significance of AI's impact on Reddit's user growth in an earnings call with analysts, and explained that the company plans to continue spending on marketing efforts intended to offset search-referral declines. "This is a consequential time for Reddit, and this period does remind me of about 11 years ago when I came back to the company," Huffman told analysts, referring to when he rejoined Reddit in 2015 following it's then-chief Ellen Pao stepping down. "We had a lot of conversations then about what is Reddit and what is Reddit for, and what is our place in this ecosystem. And we've been having those conversations at Reddit recently." "Reddit's mission is to create community and belonging for everyone in the world, and while there's overlap between Reddit and other players in the ecosystem, whether we're talking about social media or AI search, fundamentally, Reddit is communities and conversations," Huffman said.

#134. Patrick Stewart, 'Star Trek' and 'X-Men' star, dead at 86

📅 Oct 29, 2026  |  Category: Entertainment  |  Read Source →

Legendary actor Patrick Stewart, known for his roles in the "Star Trek" franchise and "X-Men" films, has died at age 86.

A post on his official Instagram page announced Steward died at his Los Angeles home on Wednesday, "in the loving care of his beloved wife Sunny, following a brief and poignant journey with Alzheimer's Disease and Lewy Body Dementia."

"He has been held in the warm embrace of family and friends, surrounded by live music, prayer, tears, laughter, and the kind of profound emotion that only he could inspire after a lifetime of devotion to us all," the post continued. "We will forever cherish the peaceful ease and reflection on his face, an enduring reminder of his invincible grace."

In lieu of flowers, the family requested that memorial contributions be made to Johns Hopkins Alzheimer's Disease Research Center Fund and Alzheimer's Foundation of America Caregiver Resources.

Born on July 13, 1940, in Mirfield, Yorkshire, England, Stewart became interested in acting at age 12, when an English teacher introduced him to William Shakespeare's writing.

Although Stewart initially dropped out of school at age 15 to work as a journalist, he quickly abandoned that pursuit and began performing in theater productions. He graduated from the Bristol Old Vic Theatre School in 1959.

At 26, Stewart joined the Royal Shakespeare Company, where he spent much of the 1960s establishing himself as a celebrated stage actor. He made his Broadway debut in 1971 in "A Midsummer Night's Dream" and would go on to perform in over a dozen stage adaptations of classics like "The Tempest" and "A Christmas Carol."

"I knew from before drama school that Shakespeare was my great love," Stewart told Playbill in 2010.

Alongside his stage work, the actor also launched his TV career in the '60s — but his big Hollywood break came two decades later, when he landed the role of Captain Jean-Luc Picard on the series "Star Trek: The Next Generation."

The sci-fi show lasted for seven seasons between 1987 and 1994. Stewart also reprised his role for an episode of the show "Star Trek: Deep Space Nine" as well as several more movies, including "Star Trek Generations" (1994), "Star Trek: First Contact" (1996), "Star Trek: Insurrection" (1998), and "Star Trek: Nemesis" (2002). He also did voice work for "Star Trek" video games.

Stewart would go on to star in another popular franchise, Sony's "X-Men," making his debut as Professor Charles Xavier, a telepathic mutant and founder of the X-Men, in the 2000 movie "X-Men."

Ian McKellen, who was also a member of the Royal Shakespeare Company in the '60s and '70s, starred opposite Stewart in the original "X-Men" series as the powerful mutant Magneto.

Despite playing onscreen rivals, the two actors became close friends in real life, with McKellen officiating Stewart's 2013 wedding to singer Sunny Ozell in Reno, Nevada.

"We became good friends as a result of shooting multimillion-dollar adventure movies," Stewart later told NPR of McKellen.

Stewart's most recent roles included voice work for the animated series "American Dad!" and the 2026 movie "The Sheep Detectives." He reprised his role as Jean-Luc Picard from 2020 to 2023 in the series "Star Trek: Picard," and returned as Charles Xavier in 2017's "Logan," 2022's "Doctor Strange in the Multiverse of Madness," and the forthcoming Marvel film "Avengers: Doomsday," out this December. The blockbuster will mark his final onscreen appearance.

Despite starring in multimillion-dollar movie franchises, Stewart returned to the stage throughout his career. He was nominated for a Tony Award for his portrayal of Macbeth in the 2008 Broadway revival and starred opposite McKellen in two-play repertory productions of "Waiting for Godot" and "No Man's Land" in 2013 and 2014.

"I have often heard actors say, 'I can't afford to go back to the theater,'" Stewart told NPR. "I look at a lot of the work that I do in film and television — which I love — but, at the same time, it underwrites and supports the other work that I want to do."

Stewart did not see his onscreen work and his stage work as at odds; on the contrary, he described his two most recognizable roles, Picard and Xavier, as "entirely complementary" to his theater career.

"The techniques are different, and the rhythm and pattern of the work are different, but the approach to how I act doesn't change — the fundamental thing is to pursue the truth," he told Playbill.

Stewart is survived by Ozell and his two children, Daniel Stewart and Sophia Stewart, from his first marriage to Sheila Falconer.

📅 Oct 29, 2026  |  Category: Technology  |  Read Source →

Intel shares plummeted more than 20% in extended trading on Thursday after the chipmaker issued a weak fourth-quarter forecast, citing continued softness in the global PC market.

Here's how the company did in the third quarter, compared with estimates from analysts polled by LSEG:

Revenue climbed 21% from $13.7 billion a year earlier, the company said in a press release.

However, revenue in Intel's client computing and physical AI group fell 5% from the same quarter a year ago to $8.1 billion, missing estimates, even as data center and AI revenue jumped 78% to $7.3 billion.

Global PC shipments declined 4% in the second quarter from a year earlier due to soaring costs for memory and other components, according to Counterpoint Research.

For the current quarter, Intel said it expects adjusted earnings per share of 25 cents on revenue between $15 billion and $16 billion. Analysts were expecting revenue of $17.1 billion and EPS of 43 cents, according to LSEG.

"We see the PC slowdown continuing at least through the first half of next year," Intel finance chief David Zinsner, said in the release. "We will continue making disciplined investments in capacity and working capital to increase supply and support future growth."

Intel's forecast reflects the effects of an explosion at a fab in Israel that killed two workers and seriously injured a third earlier this month. The incident will cost the company about $600 million in lost revenue over two quarters, Zinsner said on Thursday's earnings. CEO Lip-Bu Tan said the explosion, which he called an "unfortunate and tragic accident," caused an "extended shutdown" at the plant, impacting shipments of 10-nanometer chips.

Intel's stock has been on an upward tear, up about 150% so far this year prior to the earnings report, although it's down roughly 23% in October over growing concern over the size of the artificial intelligence buildout.

"We will remain disciplined as we invest, and we will not spend $1 of capital unless we have confidence in the health of our products and process technologies, and clear evidence of customer demand," Zinsner said on the call.

Nvidia, which has become the world's most valuable company thanks to its dominance in graphics processing units, or GPUs, is scheduled to report results in mid-November. Advanced Micro Devices, a maker of GPUs and central processing units, or CPUs, reports next week.

Known best for its industry-leading CPUs, Intel has been a beneficiary of the rise in agentic AI. While chatbots and large models rely heavily on graphics processors from Nvidia and AMD, agentic applications such as Meta's Muse and OpenAI's Dots lean on CPUs to help orchestrate tasks and data from a computer operating in the cloud.

"Despite near term headwinds affecting the broader PC industry, we continue to see robust demand for CPUs and more broadly, for all our computing platforms in the long term," Tan said in the press release.

Intel's foundry business, meanwhile, has struggled to find its footing.

Intel is the only U.S.-based manufacturer of advanced logic chips, and is now in high-volume production of its most advanced 18A chip out of its new fab in Arizona, providing an alternative to Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Co. Its next fab in Arizona is expected to begin operations by the end of 2028.

For now, Intel has no major external customers for its new chips.

"We're going to have a couple of customers ready to announce the next two quarters," Tan said on the call, adding that Intel is making "tremendous progress" on its coming 14A chips.

The Trump administration took a 10% stake in Intel last year after the company had fallen behind on its 7- and 10-nanometer technologies. Nvidia and Softbank also invested billions in Intel last year.

Tan has taken a more cautious approach to foundry spending than his predecessor, Pat Gelsinger, who launched an ambitious revival of the foundry business.

Last week, Intel said crews have installed the first structural truss at its huge Ohio fab that was originally expected to deliver chips in 2025.

In September, reports surfaced that the world's leading maker of high-bandwidth memory, SK Hynix may team up with Intel in Ohio to lease some foundry space to make memory chips at the new site. Neither company would confirm any plans.

"We continue to evaluate unique and highly differentiated opportunities to accelerate our Ohio build out, and I hope to share more on this in the near future" Tan said on the call.

Intel completed a $23 billion equity offering in August. Tech giants have spent trillions of dollars in recent years to meet insatiable demand for AI and the infrastructure needed to support it.

Tan said he's "disappointed that we were not able to meet strong customer demand in this quarter."

KONTAN.CO.ID - JAKARTA. Pemerintah tengah menggodok pemanfaatan gas bumi untuk memenuhi kebutuhan domestik. Upaya ini dilakukan melalui pembangunan infrastruktur gas, peningkatan alokasi gas untuk pasar dalam negeri, serta penyempurnaan kebijakan guna mendukung investasi di sektor minyak dan gas bumi (migas).

Direktur Pembinaan Program Minyak dan Gas Bumi Direktorat Jenderal Migas Kementerian Energi dan Sumber Daya Mineral (ESDM), Hendra Gunawan memaparkan, realisasi pemanfaatan gas bumi hingga Agustus 2026 mencapai 5.443 billion british thermal unit per day (BBTUD).

Dari jumlah tersebut, sebanyak 3.963 BBTUD atau sekitar 73% dimanfaatkan untuk kebutuhan domestik. Sementara itu, alokasi untuk ekspor mencapai 1.480 BBTUD atau sekitar 27%.

Hendra menjelaskan, salah satu pemanfaatan gas bumi di dalam negeri adalah untuk mendukung industri hilir, termasuk industri pupuk. Permintaan gas dari sektor tersebut terus meningkat seiring dengan bertambahnya pengajuan kebutuhan pasokan.

"Saat ini semakin banyak pengajuan karena permintaan gas untuk hilirisasi industri pupuk dari waktu ke waktu semakin meningkat," kata Hendra dalam acara FOREK Hulu Migas 2026 di Kempinski Grand Ballroom, Jakarta Pusat, Kamis (29/10/2026).

Selain industri pupuk, pemanfaatan gas domestik mencakup sektor transportasi, jaringan gas rumah tangga (jargas), peningkatan produksi migas, ketenagalistrikan, serta kebutuhan gas alam cair atau liquefied natural gas (LNG). Sementara, ekspor gas dilakukan dalam bentuk LNG maupun gas pipa.

Untuk mengoptimalkan pemanfaatan gas bumi, pemerintah juga mendorong pembangunan dan integrasi infrastruktur transmisi serta distribusi gas. Sejumlah proyek jaringan pipa disiapkan untuk memperluas konektivitas dan meningkatkan akses pasar terhadap pasokan gas.

Hendra bilang, pembangunan pipa transmisi Cirebon-Semarang (Cisem) menjadi salah satu proyek yang didorong pemerintah dengan menggunakan anggaran pendapatan dan belanja negara (APBN). Selain itu, pemerintah merencanakan pembangunan pipa transmisi Dumai-Sei Mangkei yang ditargetkan mulai pada 2027 untuk menghubungkan jaringan pipa di Sumatra.

Pemerintah juga merencanakan pengembangan jaringan pipa yang menghubungkan wilayah Cirebon, Batang, Solo, hingga Yogyakarta. Integrasi jaringan tersebut diharapkan memperluas penyaluran gas antardaerah.

"Ke depan, jaringan pipa transmisi dan distribusi perlu semakin terintegrasi. Kami juga mendorong pembangunan infrastruktur regasifikasi di lokasi yang dekat dengan jaringan pipa transmisi," ujar Hendra.

Selain pembangunan pipa, strategi pemerintah mencakup peningkatan alokasi LNG untuk kebutuhan domestik, pemanfaatan compressed natural gas (CNG) secara lebih luas, serta pengembangan infrastruktur gas di Indonesia Timur ,terutama yang terintegrasi dengan pembangkit listrik tenaga mesin gas (PLTMG).

Di sisi lain, pemerintah berupaya meningkatkan daya tarik investasi hulu migas melalui penyempurnaan kebijakan dan regulasi. Upaya tersebut, kata Hendra, meliputi penyesuaian skema bagi hasil kontrak kerja sama, fleksibilitas kontrak melalui skema cost recovery maupun gross split, serta pemberian insentif dengan mempertimbangkan tingkat pengembalian investasi atau internal rate of return (IRR).

Kondisi ini dinilai perlu menjadi perhatian agar pembagian nilai ekonomi di sepanjang rantai bisnis gas tetap mempertimbangkan risiko dan investasi yang ditanggung masing-masing pihak.

"Misalnya, dari hulu gas dijual US$ 7, tetapi ketika sampai ke konsumen harganya bisa mencapai US$ 14. Karena itu, kita juga perlu melihat kewajaran bisnisnya, termasuk bagaimana risiko yang ditanggung di hulu tercermin dalam keuntungan," ujar Ufo.

Ia menekankan perlunya kolaborasi antara SKK Migas, penjual gas, pemerintah, dan pelaku usaha lainnya untuk mengoptimalkan pengembangan serta pemanfaatan gas bumi. Menurutnya, gas tidak hanya perlu dikembangkan sebagai komoditas, tetapi juga sebagai penggerak kegiatan ekonomi nasional.

#142. Instagram head Adam Mosseri warns creators that AI backlash is only going to get worse

📅 Oct 29, 2026  |  Category: Technology  |  Read Source →

Instagram head Adam Mosseri said he expects anxiety and resentment about artificial intelligence to intensify over the next few years—and that social media platforms may be limited in their ability to control it.

“[The anti-AI backlash] is going to continue to get worse,” he said in a recent appearance on the Colin & Samir podcast. “I think we’re going to see more frustration and anger . . . some of it founded, some of it unfounded.”

While AI anxiety is present across many industries, Mosseri thinks it is most acute in creative fields. Among other issues, creators worry about retaining ownership of their content, whether it can be used to train AI models, and job displacement.

Concerns are especially strong among creators and influencers dependent on social media for income, who now must contend with copycat accounts and AI-generated personas. Mosseri said he has recently received many complaints about AI characters stealing stand-up comedians’ jokes.

He noted that policing this kind of activity can be incredibly difficult. 

Although Meta has long required disclosures for certain AI-generated content, and Instagram announced in August that it would restrict the reach of accounts featuring AI-generated people if they fail to self-identify, rooting out delinquents can be tricky. AI-identification technology is less than perfect and often produces false positives.

“One of the ways to really get a lot of people angry is to label real creators as AI creators by mistake,” Mosseri said.

Instagram is still perfecting how to rank AI-generated posts in users’ feeds and currently ranks AI-generated influencers no differently than humans, he added. 

Although such ranking issues are common whenever a new type of content explodes in popularity, this means human influencers are currently competing on a level playing field with virtual personalities that don’t need salaries, photo shoots, travel, or time off—and that are already eroding users’ trust in the influencer economy. (Mosseri did not comment on the podcast about whether he thinks AI influencers would take sponsorship deals away from human influencers or otherwise impact their incomes.)

Mosseri described understanding public opinion about AI as a crucial aspect of his job, noting that many young people strongly dislike the technology and that they are critical to his company’s future. 

“What I think [the backlash against AI] comes down to, when you really have a heart-to-heart with someone, is a fear of being left behind,” he said. “I want us to embrace [AI] in ways that empower people, so they don’t feel like they’re left behind.”
