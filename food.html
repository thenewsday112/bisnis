#783. Alyoshki residents went for groceries along 7-km long mined road — local resident

📅 Oct 29, 2026  |  Category: Food  |  Read Source →

SIMFEROPOL, October 29. /TASS/. The lack of food forced Alyoshki residents to go for groceries along a 7-km long mined road, resident Alexander Zakharov told TASS.

"About seven kilometers [of the road] are completely mined, and people [got out] at their own risk. Besides, there were drones before September 1, before the beginning of autumn. And later, gradually, because people had nothing to eat, they began to go to Radensk for groceries at their own risk," he said.

From 4:00 a.m. Moscow time (1:00 p.m. GMT) on October 27, Russia imposed a regime of silence in the Kherson direction to prepare for a humanitarian operation in Alyoshki in the Kherson Region. Earlier, Russia and Ukraine with the assistance of US First Lady Melania Trump agreed to a ceasefire in Alyoshki to ensure the delivery of humanitarian aid and prepare for the evacuation of civilians through a special safe corridor. Russian Presidential Spokesman Dmitry Peskov called the humanitarian situation in Alyoshki critical on September 21. It is impossible to bring food or medicines there because of shelling and drone attacks from Kiev. Of the 20,000 residents who previously lived in the town, just over 1,000 people now remain in Alyoshki.

#823. Alyoshki resident says drones constantly buzzing over town during summer

📅 Oct 29, 2026  |  Category: Food  |  Read Source →

SIMFEROPOL, October 29. /TASS/. There was a constant buzz of drones over the town of Alyoshki in the Kherson Region in the summertime, local resident Alexander Zakharov told TASS.

"Five minutes pass, and another one is flying. That constant buzzing - I now feel an automatic urge to hide when I hear a buzzing sound," he said, when asked about drone activity during the summer.

The Russian Armed Forces introduced a ceasefire in the Kherson direction at 4:00 a.m. Moscow time (1:00 a.m. GMT) on October 27 to prepare for a humanitarian operation in the town of Alyoshki. Earlier, Russia and Ukraine agreed to a ceasefire in Alyoshki, brokered by US First Lady Melania Trump, to facilitate humanitarian aid deliveries and the evacuation of civilians via a special safe corridor. On September 21, Russian Presidential Spokesman Dmitry Peskov described the situation in Alyoshki as critical, saying that food and medicine could not be delivered there amid ongoing Ukrainian shelling and drone attacks. Slightly over 1,000 residents currently remain in Alyoshki, down from a population of 20,000.

#839. Russia to continue supplying grain to Africa, Putin says

📅 Oct 29, 2026  |  Category: Food  |  Read Source →

MOSCOW, October 29. /TASS/. Russia will continue to supply grain to Africa both on a commercial basis and free of charge, President Vladimir Putin said at a press conference following the Russia-Africa Summit.

"Russia will continue to act as a responsible supplier of grain to Africa; as before, we will continue to do that both on a commercial basis and free of charge," he said.

Putin pointed out that discussions at the summit had confirmed that many African leaders viewed agriculture as the key area for cooperation with Russia. "They expect our country to continue to make a feasible contribution to strengthening food security on the African continent and supplying consumer markets with essential food products. We are certainly ready and open to that," the Russian president added.
